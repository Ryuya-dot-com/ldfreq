I learned to cook a vegetable soup with my grandmother. She placed the ingredients on the table and asked me to describe their colours and smells. While the onions softened, we talked about meals she remembered from childhood. I added the potatoes too early and had to change the cooking time. The soup still tasted good, although its texture was different from hers. Writing down what happened helped me understand that a recipe guides decisions but cannot replace observation.
