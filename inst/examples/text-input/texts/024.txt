We planned a short journey using buses rather than a car. The timetable looked simple until we noticed that some services ran only on weekdays. We compared several routes, allowed time to change buses, and chose an earlier departure. During the trip a driver suggested a quiet path beside a lake. The unexpected walk became the part we remembered best. Afterwards we compared the original plan with our experience and discussed which information would help another group make the journey.
We checked our notes and discussed the same details again. We shared our account with another class and listened to their questions. Their comments encouraged us to describe the order of events more clearly and to explain unfamiliar words.
