Our school garden began with a narrow patch of soil beside the fence. At first the ground was dry and very few plants survived. We collected leaves, mixed them into the soil, and carried water from the playground tap. A neighbour showed us how to protect young roots without covering the stems. After several weeks small green shoots appeared. Students measured the plants on different mornings and recorded the weather. Their notes helped the next group decide where to plant beans.
