On Saturday we walked along a river that crosses the town. Near the bridge the water moved quickly around stones and fallen branches. Farther downstream a broad pool reflected the trees. My friend noticed plastic bottles caught among the reeds, so we photographed the area and marked it on a map. We later joined residents who were cleaning the path. Listening to their memories showed us how the river had changed and why a familiar place deserves careful attention.
We shared our account with another class and listened to their questions. Their comments encouraged us to describe the order of events more clearly and to explain unfamiliar words.
