A small museum opened an exhibition about everyday objects. Instead of famous paintings, the room contained notebooks, toys, tickets, and tools donated by local families. Each label described how someone had used an object and why they had kept it. I spent several minutes looking at a repaired wooden chair. The visible repairs made its history easier to imagine. Our group discussed which ordinary possessions might tell future visitors something about our own lives, and we drafted labels for them.
