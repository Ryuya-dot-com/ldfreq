These short texts and metadata were authored for the ldfreq file-input tutorial.
They are MIT-licensed teaching material, not learner responses or observations.

texts/001.txt and texts/002.txt are two English documents with two lines each.
texts/003.txt is an intentionally empty document.
essays.csv contains the same three IDs and exact text strings, including LF
line endings and the final newline in each nonempty document.
metadata.csv is deliberately in a different order; join it by document_id.
japanese.txt illustrates UTF-8 input only, not English tokenization of Japanese.

Use a separate working copy for your analysis. Keep original files and their
identifiers when replacing these examples with your own permitted data.
